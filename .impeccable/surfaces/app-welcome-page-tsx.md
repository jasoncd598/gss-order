# Surface brief: public homepage (/ → app/welcome)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: makers who sell to trade, evaluating GSS's ordering portal plans (secondary to PRODUCT.md's primary users). Action: start a 14-day free trial on a plan (Stripe Checkout), or sign in. Proof: real product capabilities only; no customers, testimonials or statistics exist. Constraints: plans/prices/FAQ content and Checkout behaviour unchanged; demo photos in public/demo/ must not appear here.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A trade customer's history becomes a collection catalogue — every job GSS made hangs on the wall with its catalogue label (accession no. = plate ref, maker, date, last run) and Reorder is printed on the label itself. Refuses the category default: centred headline over a product screenshot, then a row of identical icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Enamel-blue gallery wall (#1c2b5a) owns the first viewport and the pricing room; archival grey board (#e4e2dc) for reading rooms; label stock (#f4f2ec) for catalogue labels with brass hairline frames (#b38b3b); stamp red (#a5352a) only for registration stamps tied to a real event. Bodoni Moda display, Hanken Grotesk text, Courier Prime for accession numbers and money. 2px corners, hairline rules, one index-card module.

STORY: The visitor understands GSS keeps every job its customers ordered catalogued with exact specs and price; believes reordering is as exact as reading a museum label; starts a trial or signs in.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed enamel-blue wall. Brass-ruled top bar: GSS mark left, Features / How it works / Pricing / FAQ, Sign in, brass Start free trial. Left five columns: Bodoni headline in label stock, one sentence, primary brass "Start 14-day free trial", secondary Sign in. Right seven columns: a hang of three example catalogue labels on one sightline baseline, the centre label forward with quantity and Reorder — marked "Example".

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: Pressing Reorder on the example label presses a red registration stamp onto it ("REORDER REQUESTED · date"), the label settles back into the hang. Motion: one stamp press with exponential ease-out; reduced-motion shows the stamp without movement.

FORM: The Collection Catalogue — position 7 of 7 on the grounded list; seed key 48688a18.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
